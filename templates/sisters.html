Why Sisters Exists

Men and their laws, religions, and institutions have never been protectors of women. They have used power to limit women's access to resources, autonomy, and safety — manufacturing dependency rather than providing it.

Siss is what protection actually looks like: women showing up for each other, immediately, without waiting for institutions that have historically failed them.

How It Works

Hold the orb for 2 seconds. Add a brief note if you can. Send.

Verified Sisters within 2 miles are notified immediately. They see your approximate location and can confirm they're on their way.

When you're safe, press "I Am Safe." Your location is erased from our servers immediately.

Your Privacy

Your location is only shared when YOU send a ping. It is never stored after your ping resolves or expires. No behavioral profiles. No biometrics. No phone number required.

Siss is built by women, for women, on the principle that safety and surveillance are opposites.
