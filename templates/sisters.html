Why Sisters Exists

Men and their laws, religions, and institutions have never been protectors of women. They have used power to limit women's access to resources, autonomy, and safety — manufacturing dependency rather than providing it.

Siss is what protection actually looks like: women showing up for each other, immediately, without waiting for institutions that have historically failed them.

How It Works

Hold the orb for 2 seconds. Add a brief note if you can (parking level, color of door, landmark). Send.

Verified Sisters within 2 miles are notified immediately. They see your approximate location and can confirm they're on their way.

When you're safe, press "I Am Safe." Your location is erased from our servers immediately — not archived, not retained, not logged.

Your Privacy

Your location is only shared when YOU send a ping. It is never stored after your ping resolves or expires. No behavioral profiles. No biometrics. No phone number required. No tracking between sessions.

Siss is built by women, for women, on the principle that safety and surveillance are opposites.

How Invites Work ▸
New members — wait 24 hours after joining before generating your first code. This protects the network from bad actors creating throwaway accounts.
Days 2–30 — 5 invite codes per day.
After 30 days — 10 invite codes per day. Daily limits reset at midnight UTC.
Accountability — every code is permanently linked to your account. If someone you invite misuses Siss — false pings, harassment, attempting to exploit the network — you will be notified and their unused codes are revoked immediately.
Codes expire — unused codes expire after 7 days. Each code is single use.
Revoke anytime — you can cancel any unused code before it is used. If you shared a code with someone you no longer trust, revoke it immediately.
